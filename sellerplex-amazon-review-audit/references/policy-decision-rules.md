# Manual QA rules for removal candidates

The objective is not to remove negative opinions. A candidate needs both a 1-3 star rating and an identifiable Amazon Community Guidelines violation.

## Strong textual signals

- Review focuses only on seller, customer service, order, return, delivery, freight, shipping packaging, or delivery damage.
- Private information such as email, phone, mailing address, order number, or other identifiers.
- External or affiliate links.
- Repetitive text, gibberish, symbol spam, or ASCII art.
- Profanity, threats, harassment, hate speech, or explicit sexual content.
- Unsupported marketplace language.
- Explicit promotional, compensated, incentivized, or conflict-of-interest disclosure.
- Encouragement of illegal activity or dangerous product misuse.
- Prohibited claims about preventing or curing serious medical conditions.

## Do not auto-recommend action

- Negative sentiment, low rating, or harsh product criticism alone.
- Product failure after use.
- A non-verified purchase by itself.
- A Vine review.
- Mentioning or comparing another brand without promotional purpose.
- Mixed content that includes both delivery/seller complaints and substantive product evaluation.
- Suspected fake, competitor, or incentivized behavior without evidence in the review or supporting metadata.

Every action recommendation should preserve the exact offending excerpt, policy category, confidence, review ID, review URL, ASIN, star, date, and a concise rationale. Ambiguous or mixed reviews belong in manual review.
